Triple-positive breast cancer: choosing the first treatment

1

What we found

Your cancer
Menopause

The cancer has spread beyond the breast. It has two drivers we can target: HER2, an accelerator, and estrogen, a fuel. That is why it is called triple-positive.

Cancer that has spread usually can't be cured. It can often be controlled for years. The goal is to keep it under control as long as possible while you feel as well as possible.

In the study that set today's standard, CLEOPATRA, about 37 in 100 people were still alive 8 years later. Treatments have improved since then.

2

How these medicines work

Trastuzumab (Herceptin) and pertuzumab (Perjeta) are antibodies that block HER2 from two sides. Together they are called HP. They can be given in a vein or as one shot under the skin (Phesgo).

Taxane chemo (docetaxel or paclitaxel) shrinks the cancer quickly. With HP it is called THP.

T-DXd (trastuzumab deruxtecan, Enhertu) is trastuzumab with chemo attached. It carries the chemo straight into HER2 cells.

An aromatase inhibitor (letrozole, anastrozole or exemestane) lowers estrogen. Palbociclib (Ibrance) is a pill that slows cell division.

  • No study has compared all three plans with each other. Each pair of bars comes from its own study.
  • PATINA started counting after 4 to 8 doses of chemo (about 3 to 6 months), and only for people whose cancer was controlled by the chemo. DESTINY-Breast09 started counting on the first day of treatment. So the PATINA bars sit later and can't be lined up exactly with the others.

5

Side effects to weigh

Numbers are how many people out of 100 had each side effect, at any level, in DESTINY-Breast09 (T-DXd + pertuzumab or THP) and in PATINA after chemo (with or without palbociclib). Most were mild to moderate. Tell us early about any side effect: a lower dose or a break usually helps.

6

If the first treatment stops working

After chemo + HP

T-DXd is usually next. In DESTINY-Breast03, given after trastuzumab and a taxane, it kept the cancer under control for a median of about 29 months, versus about 7 months with the older drug T-DM1.

After T-DXd + pertuzumab

Good options remain, such as tucatinib with trastuzumab and capecitabine, T-DM1, or chemo with HP. How well they work after T-DXd is still being studied.

Early results from DESTINY-Breast09 show the cancer also stayed under control longer through the next treatment when T-DXd + pertuzumab came first.

Most people with this cancer go on to have several lines of treatment. Choosing the first one is partly choosing the order.

Also

Call us right away if you have

  • A fever of 100.4°F (38°C) or higher, or chills
  • A new or worse cough, shortness of breath, or chest pain (call 911 if severe)
  • Diarrhea 4 or more times a day, or you can't keep fluids down
  • Swelling in your legs, or trouble breathing when lying flat
  • New headaches, confusion, weakness, or trouble with balance
