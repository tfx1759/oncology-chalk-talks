Surgery removed the cancer. Your cancer is hormone-sensitive: it uses estrogen to grow.

Your tumor gene test score is low. For women past menopause, a score of 25 or lower means chemo would not add benefit.

So the hormone pill is your main treatment. It lowers the chance the cancer comes back.

2

How hormone pills work

Estrogen is fuel for this cancer. After menopause, the ovaries stop, and most estrogen is made in body fat by an enzyme called aromatase.

Aromatase inhibitors (anastrozole, letrozole, exemestane) shut down that factory, so there is less fuel.

Tamoxifen lets estrogen be made but blocks it from plugging into the cancer cell.

Either way, any hidden cancer cells are starved of the signal they need to grow.

Numbers are rough, over 5 years, from the large trials comparing these pills (ATAC, BIG 1-98). “Added risk” means on top of the usual risk for someone your age. Most side effects ease after the first few months. If one pill doesn't suit you, switching to another aromatase inhibitor or to tamoxifen often helps, so tell us rather than stopping. 6

Should we add a CDK4/6 inhibitor?

CDK4/6 inhibitors are pills that put the brakes on cell division. Added to a hormone pill, they lower the chance of recurrence for cancers with a higher risk, mostly when lymph nodes are involved.

Ribociclib (Kisqali), 3 years

NATALEE trial. Taken with an aromatase inhibitor; 3 weeks on, 1 week off.
  • Who was in it: stage II–III. Any lymph node with cancer, or no nodes but a tumor over 5 cm, or 2–5 cm and grade 3, or grade 2 with Ki-67 20% or more or a high gene score.
  • At 5 years, 85.5 of 100 were cancer-free versus 81.0 with the hormone pill alone: about 4 to 5 more per 100.
  • Low white blood counts, liver test changes, heart rhythm check (ECG), tiredness. Blood tests every month at first.

Abemaciclib (Verzenio), 2 years

monarchE trial. Taken twice a day with any hormone pill.
  • Who was in it: 4 or more lymph nodes with cancer, or 1–3 nodes plus a tumor 5 cm or larger or grade 3.
  • At 7 years, 77.4 of 100 were cancer-free versus 70.9: about 6 to 7 more per 100. Also about 2 more per 100 alive (86.8 versus 85.0).
  • Diarrhea in most people, usually early and manageable. Low blood counts, tiredness, blood clots in about 2–3 in 100.

The benefits above are for the higher-risk women in those trials. When the risk of recurrence is lower, the benefit is smaller, while the side effects stay the same.

Call us if you have

  • Pain, swelling or redness in one leg, or sudden shortness of breath or chest pain (call 911)
